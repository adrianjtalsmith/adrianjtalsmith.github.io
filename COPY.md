### Bio

I am a Senior Lecturer in Philosophy at [King's College London](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/people/adrian-alsmith). Most of my work concerns bodily self-consciousness: how the body is mentally represented, how we locate ourselves in the world we experience, and what it is to experience a body as one's own. A recurring theme is the ways in which our experience is structured by bodily and mental activity. That theme has led me more recently to the relation between perception and imagination, and to the ways in which people think about, talk about, and design generative artificial systems.

I joined King's in 2020. Before that I held research positions at LOGOS (University of Barcelona), the Jean Nicod Institute (École Normale Supérieure), and the Centre for Subjectivity Research (University of Copenhagen). I took my PhD at the University of Mainz, supervised by Thomas Metzinger, as part of the DISCOS Marie Curie RTN and the CONTACT ESF project, after taking an MSc. in Mind, Language and Embodied Cognition at the University of Edinburgh and a BA in Philosophy & Psychology at the University of Warwick.

I co-edit the Philosophy and Cognitive Science section of the [MIT Open Encyclopedia of Cognitive Science](https://oecs.mit.edu/). Previously, I was an Associate Editor of *Analysis* and of *Philosophy and the Mind Sciences*, and Philosophy Programme Chair of the European Society for Philosophy and Psychology.

### COLLABORATION WITH GRASPINGAI

[GraspingAI](https://www.graspingai.com/), founded by Aaron Kagan and David Pearl, is a community of philosophers, designers, and user-experience researchers thinking about AI together. My involvement grew out of a collaboration with Aaron on anthropomorphism, artificial selfhood, and interface design, which began during his research visit to King's in 2022. We have written three pieces together.

**Talk to Me Like a Human.** With Aaron Kagan. GraspingAI, August 2026. Based on my [Elizans paper](#publications). [Read at GraspingAI](https://www.graspingai.com/thinking/talk-to-me-like-a-human/)

**Metaphors We AI By.** With Allen Bevans, David Pearl, and Aaron Kagan. *Deep Context* Substack, March 2026. [Read on Substack](https://deepcontext.substack.com/p/metaphors-we-ai-by)
